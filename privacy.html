Privacy Policy for KeyPlay

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

KeyPlay is developed by Dan Kim ("I", "me"). This policy explains what KeyPlay does with your information. The short version: KeyPlay does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to me or to anyone else.

1. Information I Collect

None. KeyPlay has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. I never receive your photos, sounds, settings, or any other data from the app.

2. What Stays on Your Device

KeyPlay saves a few things locally, inside the app's private storage on your device. Nothing here is sent to me.

  • Background photo: When you pick a photo, KeyPlay uses the iOS system photo picker. KeyPlay never sees the rest of your photo library. The app saves a separate copy of the one photo you choose as a file in its own private storage. Deleting the original from your library does not remove this copy, and removing the copy does not affect your original.
  • Custom sound: If you choose your own audio file, KeyPlay saves a copy of it in the same private storage.
  • Settings: Your text color is saved on your device using the standard iOS app preferences (UserDefaults).

3. Motion Data

KeyPlay reads your device's accelerometer only to detect when you shake the phone, so it can play the jingle. This motion data is processed in real time on your device. It is not recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

4. Backups

Because these files live in the app's storage, iOS may include them in your device or iCloud backups, depending on your own iOS settings. That is handled by Apple on your behalf under your Apple account. I do not have access to those backups.

5. Third Parties

KeyPlay does not share data with third parties and contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code.

6. Children

KeyPlay does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Your Control and Deleting Your Data

  • Tap RESET in the app to delete the saved background photo.
  • Delete the app to permanently remove all photos, sounds, and settings that KeyPlay saved on your device. Copies already included in an iCloud or device backup are managed through your Apple account settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, I will update the "Last Updated" date above. If KeyPlay ever begins collecting any data, this policy will be updated before that change ships.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email me at: this_dude.keyplay@manyme.com
