Common questions

How do I change the background photo?

Tap the + button at the bottom right and choose a photo. Tap RESET to remove it.

How do I use my own sound?

Long-press the speaker button and choose an audio file. Tap the speaker to play it. With VoiceOver, use the actions rotor on the speaker button and choose "Choose sound file".

The shake doesn't trigger the sound.

Make sure the volume is up. KeyPlay plays even when the silent switch is on. Shake a little harder, or use the on-screen SHAKE THE PHONE button.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. See the privacy policy.
