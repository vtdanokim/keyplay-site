Common questions

How do I change the background photo?

Tap the + button at the bottom right and choose a photo. Tap RESET to remove it.

How do I use my own song?

Tap SONG and choose an audio file from the Files app, for example your team's fight song. After that, tap SONG to play or stop it, and touch and hold SONG to choose a different file. Shaking always plays the key jingle. With VoiceOver, use the actions rotor on the SONG button and choose "Choose song file".

The shake doesn't trigger the sound.

Make sure the volume is up. KeyPlay plays even when the silent switch is on. Shake a little harder, or use the on-screen SHAKE THE PHONE button.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. See the privacy policy.
